# What Is an AI Operating System or AI OS

## Summary
The article explores the concept of an AI operating system (AI OS) by drawing parallels with traditional data operating systems. It explains that just as a conventional operating system abstracts away the complexities of hardware and infrastructure for developers, an AI OS aims to do the same for artificial intelligence and operational knowledge within companies. The author, Animesh Kumar, discusses how DataOS was developed to solve the problem of duplicated effort in building data stacks across organizations, leading to the idea that an AI OS could similarly streamline the development and maintenance of AI systems.

## Key Concepts
- **AI Operating System**: A system that abstracts away the complexities of infrastructure for AI-related operations.
- **DataOS**: The first operating system designed specifically for data management, which serves as a precursor to modern AI OS concepts.
- **Scalability Through Autonomy**: How an AI OS can provide autonomy to business teams, enabling them to operate independently without needing their own dedicated infrastructure.
