# Building AI Strategy on Sand

## Summary
The article discusses the critical role of concept management and knowledge representation in building effective AI strategies. It emphasizes that while large language models are often at the forefront of AI discussions, getting concepts wrong can lead to incorrect answers from these models, which is an expensive failure hard to detect. The piece targets leaders and managers who need to understand why accurate concept definitions are foundational to successful AI implementations.

## Key Concepts
- **Concept Management**: The process of defining clear, consistent, and well-defined concepts.
- **Knowledge Representation**: Describing reality in a form that both humans and machines can understand and use.
- **Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG)**: An AI model searching data to compose an answer, relying on the quality of underlying data for accuracy.
- **Model Context Protocol (MCP)**: Directly connecting models to systems for real-time data retrieval.
