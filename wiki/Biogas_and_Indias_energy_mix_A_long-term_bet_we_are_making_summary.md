# Biogas and India’s Energy Mix: A Long-Term Bet We Are Making

## Summary
The article discusses the potential of biogas as a renewable alternative to imported natural gas in India. It highlights that over 50% of India's natural gas consumption comes from imports, which could be significantly reduced through the use of domestically produced biogas. The author points out that replacing just 20% of natural gas consumption with biogas could save approximately $29 billion on import bills between FY25 and FY30. The piece explores different tiers of biogas technology and provides examples, such as decentralized biogas systems in rural areas and waste-to-energy facilities in urban settings.

## Key Concepts
- Biogas as a renewable energy source
- Importance of reducing reliance on imported natural gas
- Decentralized vs. centralized biogas models
- Case studies from Akshayakalpa, Hasiru Dala Innovations, and Nexus Novus
