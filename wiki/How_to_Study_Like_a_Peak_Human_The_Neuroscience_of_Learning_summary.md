# How to Study Like a Peak Human: The Neuroscience of Learning

## Summary
This article delves into the cognitive science behind effective studying, emphasizing that ease and comfort do not equate to learning. It introduces key concepts such as active recall and highlights how struggling with information during study sessions actually aids in memory formation. The author shares personal experiences and practical techniques for mastering complex subjects more efficiently.

## Key Concepts
- **Active Recall**: Testing oneself on material without looking at the source, which is significantly more effective than re-reading or reviewing notes.
- **Struggle Signal**: The brain releases dopamine and norepinephrine when struggling to recall information, reinforcing memory pathways.
- **Desirable Difficulty**: Making study sessions challenging helps in building stronger neural connections.
