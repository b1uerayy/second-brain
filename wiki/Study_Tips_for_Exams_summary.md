# Study Tips for Exams

## Summary
This summary provides practical and high-impact study tips aimed at enhancing retention and understanding during exam preparation. It emphasizes active recall, focus cycles, spaced repetition, solving problems, utilizing past year questions (PYQs), breaking down topics into manageable chunks, fixing your environment, daily revision, and several underrated techniques like the blurting method, interleaving, and more.

## Key Concepts
- **Active Recall + Spaced Repetition**
- **Focus Cycles (50–10 Rule)**
- **Solving Problems Instead of Reading Theory**
- **Using PYQs to Identify Repeated Topics**
- **Breaking Big Topics into Small Wins**
- **Fixing Your Study Environment**
- **Daily Revision Before Sleep**
- **Energy vs. Time Management**
- **Underrated Techniques (Blurting Method, Interleaving, etc.)**
