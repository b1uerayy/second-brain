# AI Knowledge Base Built on Karpathy’s LLM Wiki Method

## Summary
This article discusses the importance of efficiently managing and organizing information, especially for those handling large amounts of data. The author emphasizes that saving articles, documents, and insights without proper organization leads to a situation where useful knowledge is either difficult to find or fragmented. A method proposed by Andrej Karpathy, known as the "LLM Wiki Method," aims to compile knowledge once and allow AI tools to build a structured wiki from it, making retrieval and integration of information more efficient.

## Key Concepts
- **Knowledge Compilation**: The process of compiling useful information into a structured format.
- **AI Tool Integration**: Utilizing AI tools to automate the organization of compiled knowledge.
- **Efficiency in Knowledge Management**: Reducing the time spent on organizing notes by automating parts of the process with AI.
