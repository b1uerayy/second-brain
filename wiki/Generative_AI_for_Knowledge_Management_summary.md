# Generative AI for Knowledge Management

## Summary
The article discusses the potential of generative AI models in knowledge management use cases. It emphasizes that businesses should collaborate with a trusted partner to effectively deploy these technologies, ensuring successful integration and utilization.

## Key Concepts
- **Generative AI**: A type of artificial intelligence capable of generating new content.
- **Knowledge Management**: The process of creating, sharing, storing, retrieving, and maintaining organizational knowledge.
- **Trusted Partner**: An entity that can provide guidance and support in the deployment of generative AI models for businesses.
