Here are some *underrated but high-impact* study tricks:

---

## 🧠 1. Blurting Method (Insanely Effective)

After studying a topic:

- Close everything
- Take a blank page
- **Write everything you remember**

Then compare with notes.

👉 This exposes exactly what you *don’t know*, not what you think you know.

---

## 🔁 2. Interleaving (Mix Topics on Purpose)

Instead of:

- 3 hours only DSA

Do:

- 1 hr DSA
- 1 hr OS
- 1 hr DSA again

👉 Your brain learns to *switch contexts*, which improves problem-solving (especially for exams).

---

## ❌ 3. Study What You Avoid First

Be honest—there’s always that one topic you keep delaying.

Do it **first thing in the day**.

👉 Your brain is fresh + you remove mental stress early.

---

## 🗣️ 4. “Explain Like I’m Dumb” Technique

Pick a topic and explain it like:

- You’re teaching a 10-year-old
- No jargon allowed

If you get stuck → weak understanding.

👉 This is basically how toppers revise fast.

---

## 🧪 5. Test Before You Study

Sounds backward, but:

- Try solving questions *before* learning the topic

👉 This creates curiosity and your brain remembers better when you later study it.

---

## ⏳ 6. Use “Ugly Notes” (Not Perfect Notes)

Stop wasting time making aesthetic notes.

Instead:

- Rough diagrams
- Arrows
- Keywords

👉 Pretty notes = time waste  
👉 Ugly notes = faster learning

---

## 🎯 7. The 2-Minute Rule (Kills Procrastination)

If you feel lazy:

- Just study for **2 minutes**

Most of the time, you’ll continue.

👉 Starting is the hardest part, not studying.

---

## 🔄 8. Same-Day + Next-Day Combo

Most people revise after a week → too late.

Do:

- Quick revision same day (10 mins)
- Proper revision next day

👉 This locks memory much stronger.

---

## 🧍 9. Change Study Mode (When Stuck)

If you’re bored:

- Switch from reading → solving
- Sitting → standing
- Silent → explaining aloud

👉 Keeps brain active without quitting.

---

## 📉 10. Track “Actual Work,” Not Time

Don’t say:

> “I studied 5 hours”

Track:

- Problems solved
- Topics completed

👉 Output matters, not sitting time.

---

## ⚡ 11. Use Micro-Deadlines

Instead of:

> “I’ll study CN today”

Say:

> “Finish TCP + 10 PYQs in 45 mins”

👉 Pressure = focus

---

## 🧩 12. Memory Hooks (Super Useful for Exams)

Turn boring info into:

- Acronyms
- Weird connections

Example:  
OSI layers → **Please Do Not Throw Sausage Pizza Away**

👉 Makes recall faster in exams.

👉 You’re basically studying for nothing long-term.
