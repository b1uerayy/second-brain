As someone who often handles large amounts of information, you’ve probably had this experience:

- You come across a great article and quickly save it to your bookmarks.
- You see a useful document and download it locally.
- You encounter a brilliant insight and copy it into your note-taking app.

Then these items just sit there quietly, never to be looked at again.

Our bookmark folders, note-taking apps, and cloud drives are filled with materials labeled “might be useful later.” But when we actually need them, we either can’t find them or we only find scattered fragments that can’t be pieced together into useful knowledge.

Let alone spending time manually organizing—organizing notes often takes longer than reading, so in the end, I just give up.
